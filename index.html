Research Interests

Modern AI systems can solve increasingly complex problems, but we still don't fully understand when to trust their decisions, especially in high-stakes domains like healthcare. I'm interested in understanding how these systems reason, why they fail, and how we can make them more reliable. I explore these questions through causal inference and reasoning in large language models.
